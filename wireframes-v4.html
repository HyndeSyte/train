Audio enable — no screen
Reversed: the Sound Unlock gate is removed. Audio unlocks silently on the "Start Today's Session" tap — that tap is the gesture iOS requires, so no screen is needed. In-the-moment mute is the speaker icon in the session header (see the Work / Rest timer frames). Persistent on/off lives in Settings → Sound. Less friction, more intuitive.
